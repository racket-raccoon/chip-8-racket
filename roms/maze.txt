Non-default setup for maze.ch8

Change the execution speed:

  opcodes/second = 900

Keep the current compatibility parameters. This hash is David Winter's
alternate Maze ROM, with original CHIP-8 as its preferred platform. That
platform defaults to 15 opcodes per 60 Hz frame (900 per second).

Modern CHIP-8 is also listed as a fallback. The original COSMAC VIP vblank
wait is not configurable in this emulator, so the timing is approximate.
