Setup for space-invaders.ch8

Use the SUPER-CHIP 1.1 (quirks only) preset (1800 opcodes/second).

This David Winter ROM targets SUPER-CHIP 1.1, whose database default is 30
opcodes per 60 Hz frame. At 600 per second its startup and invader formation
are drawn in slow, visibly separate chunks. Some short execution paths happen
to produce the same state with original CHIP-8 quirks, but that is not a
reliable compatibility profile for the complete game.
