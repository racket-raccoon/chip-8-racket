Non-default setup for kaleidoscope.ch8

Change the execution speed:

  opcodes/second = 900

Keep the current compatibility parameters. This is Joseph Weisbecker's
1978 original-CHIP-8 Kaleidoscope ROM. Its preferred platform default is
15 opcodes per 60 Hz frame (900 per second).

The database also lists modern CHIP-8 as a fallback, but original CHIP-8
is preferred. The original COSMAC VIP vblank wait is not configurable in
this emulator, so the timing is approximate.
