Setup for kaleidoscope.ch8

Use the Original CHIP-8 preset (900 opcodes/second).

This is Joseph Weisbecker's 1978 original-CHIP-8 Kaleidoscope ROM. The
preset includes its preferred platform default of 15 opcodes per 60 Hz frame
and the original COSMAC VIP vblank draw wait. The database also lists modern
CHIP-8 as a fallback, but original CHIP-8 is preferred.
