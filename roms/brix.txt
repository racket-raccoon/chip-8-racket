Change the execution speed:

  opcodes/second = 900

Keep the current compatibility parameters. This ROM's SHA-1 matches Brix
by Andreas Gustafsson, classified as original CHIP-8. The platform default
is 15 opcodes per 60 Hz frame (900 per second).

Note: the original COSMAC VIP also waited for vblank after drawing. This
emulator has no parameter for that behavior, so the timing is approximate.
